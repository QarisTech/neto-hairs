How do I detangle my wig?

Start at the ends and gently work upwards with a wide-tooth comb. Follow the care instructions for your specific texture.

Can I use heat on my wig?

Check the product’s heat guidance first. For heat-safe hair, use a suitable protectant and the lowest effective temperature.

How should I store my hair?

Let the hair dry fully, then keep it on a wig stand or in a clean satin bag. Avoid storing wet hair or leaving it near direct heat.

Where can I find delivery information?

Neto Hairs is based in Nigeria. Delivery areas, fees and return terms will be confirmed before the store opens for orders.
